# ProofForge 8 Pillars of Digital Taste

1. **Subtlety:** Good UI whispers, bad UI shouts. Never over-saturate accents.
2. **Intentional Hierarchy:** The most important action is unmistakable without being loud.
3. **Restraint:** Cut at least 20% of decorative elements in the first review pass.
4. **Natural Physics:** Transitions ease smoothly (spring or cubic-bezier), never linear.
5. **Precision Alignment:** Every baseline, margin, and icon center aligns mathematically.
6. **Depth through Layers:** Create z-axis depth using translucent panels (`backdrop-blur`) and borders, not heavy drop-shadows.
7. **Spatial Rhythm:** Generous breathing room gives components room to communicate.
8. **Responsive Fluidity:** UI feels deliberate at 320px mobile just as it does on a 4K display.
